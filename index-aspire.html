Royal Concierge Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Essential information for planning your retreat to The Ehelepola Hotel.

What is the check-in and check-out protocol?

Check-in commences from 2:00 PM with traditional Kandyan oil lamp lighting and chilled King Coconut water. Check-out is at 11:00 AM. Guaranteed early check-in or late checkout can be arranged via your personal royal butler.

How do we arrange helicopter or luxury limousine transfers?

Our private on-estate helipad accommodates twin-engine private aircraft. Flights from Colombo International Airport (CMB) take approximately 30 minutes. Chauffeur transfers in our private Mercedes-Benz S-Class fleet can also be arranged directly through our concierge.

Are dietary restrictions and Ayurvedic meal plans accommodated?

Every meal is tailored to guest preferences. Our executive culinary team consults directly with our resident Ayurvedic doctor to craft bespoke menus aligning with your dosha balance, vegan, gluten-free, or halal preferences.
