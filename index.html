HERITAGE BOUTIQUE RETREAT

Ayubowan To Heritage Ehelepola

Step into a sanctuary where timeless aristocratic elegance meets the enchanting serenity of Sri Lanka's historic hill capital. Nestled amidst the verdant misty hills and the storied heritage of Ehelepolawaththa in Gurudeniya, Heritage Ehelepola Hotel is an intimate luxury retreat that seamlessly blends noble Kandyan history with gracious contemporary comfort.

Our boutique sanctuary is thoughtfully designed to embrace the tranquil harmony of its hillside surroundings, offering panoramic mountain vistas, exquisite antique architecture, and an ambiance of effortless peace. Every detail—from our handcrafted royal suites and private garden pavilions to our attentive personalized butler service—is crafted to indulge the discerning traveler seeking both pure luxury and authentic cultural wonder.

At Heritage Ehelepola, our dedicated team ensures your every comfort is attended to with genuine warmth, allowing you to unwind and immerse yourself in a world of calm and rejuvenation. Whether you join us for a restful weekend escape or an extended cultural retreat, you will be welcomed with authentic Kandyan hospitality and the warmest "Ayubowan" that makes you feel truly at home.

BESPOKE HIGHLAND EXPEDITIONS

An Exquisite Collection Of Experiences

Curated exclusively for guests of Heritage Ehelepola Hotel. Each private excursion includes dedicated chauffeur transport, personal butler arrangements, and bespoke timings crafted around your personal desires.

PANORAMIC ADVENTURE
Gampola • 28 km from Hotel (45 Mins)

Ambuluwawa Biodiversity Peak

Ascend the iconic white spiral tower perched 3,567 ft above sea level. Experience 360-degree vistas across Adams Peak and mist-shrouded mountain ridges surrounded by rare biodiversity flora.

Half-Day Excursion
ROYAL CULTURAL HERITAGE
Medawala • 14 km from Hotel (30 Mins)

14th-Century Embekke Wood Carvings

Explore an ancient masterpiece of Kandyan Gampola kingdom heritage. Marvel at 514 intricate wood carvings including the legendary wrestlers, double-headed eagle, swans, and lotus rosettes carved into sacred ironwood pillars.

2.5 Hours
HIGHLAND RAILWAY
Central Highlands • Station Chauffeur Pick-up

Iconic Ceylon Blue Train Odyssey

Celebrated globally as one of the most picturesque train rides in the world. Glide past sweeping Ceylon tea fields, cascading waterfalls, and colonial stone viaducts with reserved observation class seats.

Full-Day / Flexible
SPIRITUAL SERENITY
Kandy Valley • 8 km from Hotel (15 Mins)

Sacred Hillside Temples & White Stupa

Immerse yourself in serenity amidst Kandy's tranquil hillside shrines and sacred white stupas. Enjoy peaceful meditation, soft evening chanting, and panoramic golden sunset views over the misty mountain valley.

2 Hours • Sunset Ideal
