The Noble Heritage

An Aristocratic Legacy in the Kingdom of Kandy

Named in honor of the storied Maha Nilame (Prime Minister) of the historic Kandyan court, The Ehelepola Hotel stands as a restored masterpiece where 19th-century royal grandeur seamlessly converges with contemporary five-star indulgence.

Set within an 18-acre private estate bordered by virgin tea plantations and ancient spice groves, our retreat offers discerning travelers an unparalleled haven of privacy, bespoke butler service, and unhurried luxury.

Frequently Answered Inquiries

What are the check-in and check-out times?
Official check-in is at 2:00 PM and check-out is at 12:00 PM. Early check-in and guaranteed late check-out can be arranged complimentary for guests staying in our Royal Suite or Governor's Verandah upon availability.
How do we reach the resort from Colombo Airport?
We provide private chauffeur service in luxury Mercedes-Benz and BMW vehicles (approx. 2.5 hours via the Central Expressway). Alternatively, our concierge can charter a direct 30-minute helicopter flight landing on our private estate helipad.
Are children permitted at The Ehelepola Hotel?
To preserve an atmosphere of profound serenity and meditative peace, we warmly welcome children aged 10 and above in our suites. Custom family stays can be arranged with prior concierge notice.
Can special dietary or vegan meals be accommodated?
Absolutely. Our executive culinary team specializes in bespoke vegan, gluten-free, halal, and individualized Ayurvedic dosha diets formulated with fresh organic produce from our estate gardens.
