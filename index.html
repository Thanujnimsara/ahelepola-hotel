DISCOVER HERITAGE EHELEPOLA

A Timeless Sanctuary of Regal Serenity & Mountain Mist

Rooted in the storied heritage of the Kandyan kingdom, Heritage Ehelepola Hotel is an intimate boutique retreat where regal history blends seamlessly with tranquil hillside nature.

Wake to cool highland breezes, the gentle chorus of dawn songbirds amidst ancient mango canopies, and the heartfelt warmth of authentic Sri Lankan hospitality. Handcrafted teak furniture, century-old heirlooms, and expansive garden verandas create a rare sanctuary far removed from modern distractions.

Royal Botanical Gardens

Century-old royal flora, towering palm avenues, orchid houses, and vibrant floral pergolas along the Mahaweli River in Peradeniya.

Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic

The sacred spiritual epicenter of Sri Lanka and ancient royal palace complex of Kandyan monarchs, overlooking the serene waters of Kandy Lake.

DINING AT HERITAGE EHELEPOLA RESORT

Delectable Excellence

Step into our heritage dining spaces, where every dish is an artful celebration of flavor and royal Ceylon hospitality. Our chefs blend vibrant Sri Lankan spices and freshly handpicked hill-country garden herbs with global culinary influences to create meals that excite your taste buds and warm your soul. From fiery favorites and crispy morning hoppers to subtly seasoned delights, breezy courtyard terrace lunches, and candlelit garden feasts, we serve food that's as lively and enchanting as our estate atmosphere. Whether you're sharing a family luncheon, enjoying a romantic dinner, or simply craving something unforgettable, Heritage Ehelepola is your perfect sanctuary for a feast that cures every craving.
